# Example turns

These come from the conversation this skill was designed in. Match their feel: short context, visible assumptions, clear options, one recommendation tied to what the user said.

## A question with perspectives

> **Q1 · Offline mode**
> **Context:** Users on trains lose connection. This decides the data layer.
> **Perspectives:**
> - *Advocate:* losing work is the top frustration.
> - *Architect:* full sync roughly doubles backend complexity.
> - *Skeptic:* sync conflicts are where data loss actually happens.
>
> **Options:**
> - **A) Online only.** Simplest, but breaks on trains.
> - **B) Read offline.** Cached content, no edits without connection. Moderate effort.
> - **C) Full offline sync.** Best experience, needs conflict handling, a lot of work.
>
> ➡️ **B:** fits your "keep it lean" goal and covers the common case. C can come later.
